### Sample input and expected output (illustrative)

The PDF uses `customer_snapshots`, with `cust_id` as the natural key and both `updated_at` and `version` to identify the newest row. This table is absent from its shared seven-table schema.

**Input — `customer_snapshots`**:

| cust_id | updated_at | version | value |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 2026-01-01 | 1 | old A |
| 1 | 2026-02-01 | 1 | newer A |
| 1 | 2026-02-01 | 2 | newest A |
| 2 | 2026-01-15 | 4 | old B |
| 2 | 2026-03-01 | 1 | new B |

**Expected output** (one row per key: latest `updated_at`, then greatest `version` if timestamps tie):

| cust_id | updated_at | version | value |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 2026-02-01 | 2 | newest A |
| 2 | 2026-03-01 | 1 | new B |
