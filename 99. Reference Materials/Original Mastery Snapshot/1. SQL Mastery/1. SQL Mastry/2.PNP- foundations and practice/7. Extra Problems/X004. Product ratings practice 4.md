### Sample input and expected output (illustrative)

For this illustration, rating bands are `< 3`, `>= 3 and < 4`, `>= 4`, and unrated (`NULL`). A product with no order details has 0 units sold.

**Input — `Products`**:

| ProductID | Rating |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2.5 |
| 2 | 3.5 |
| 3 | 4.0 |
| 4 | NULL |
| 5 | 4.5 |

**Input — `OrderDetails`**:

| OrderDetailID | ProductID | Quantity |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 11 | 1 | 2 |
| 12 | 2 | 4 |
| 13 | 3 | 6 |
| 14 | 5 | 2 |

**Expected output** (average *units per product* in each band):

| rating_band | product_count | avg_units_per_product |
|---|---:|---:|
| below 3 | 1 | 2.00 |
| 3 to below 4 | 1 | 4.00 |
| 4 and above | 2 | 4.00 |
| unrated | 1 | 0.00 |
