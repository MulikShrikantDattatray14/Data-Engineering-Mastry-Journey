|---:|---:|
| 1 | 100 |
| 2 | 90 |
| 3 | 90 |
| 4 | 80 |

**Expected output** when salaries are sorted high to low and tied rows use `emp_id` for a repeatable row-number display:

| emp_id | salary | row_number | salary_rank | dense_rank |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 100 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 90 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 90 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| 4 | 80 | 4 | 4 | 3 |

Only salary decides the tied `RANK` and `DENSE_RANK` values.
