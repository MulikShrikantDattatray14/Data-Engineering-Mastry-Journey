### Sample input and expected output (illustrative)

**Input — `orders`**:

| order_id | order_date | amount |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | 2026-01-01 | 10 |
| 2 | 2026-01-03 | 20 |
| 3 | 2026-01-07 | 40 |

There are no orders on January 2, 4, 5, or 6; those calendar days have revenue 0. The illustrated report ends on January 7.

**Expected output** for completed seven-calendar-day windows:

| window_end | seven_day_total | seven_day_average |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2026-01-07 | 70 | 10.00 |

The seven-day total is 10 + 0 + 20 + 0 + 0 + 0 + 40 = 70; dividing by seven calendar days gives 10.00.
